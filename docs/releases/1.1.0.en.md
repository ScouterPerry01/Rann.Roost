# RANN's Roost 1.1.0

## New

- **Try a sample household.** On the welcome screen, **Open a sample household** opens one of three ready-made households to explore the app without entering anything: Alex and Sam (a couple with a child, a rental and a side business), a family of five (a mortgage, an inherited cottage, daycare, activities and large portfolios) or a young single person learning to budget and starting to invest. Choose English or French and any province or territory: names, places, prices, taxes and benefits follow. A sample is a throw-away copy: it is never backed up or added to your recent households, and it is deleted when you lock or close it.
- **Calendar at the top of the menu.** The Calendar now stands on its own at the top of the menu, under Contacts, instead of inside the Money group, so it is always one click away.

## Fixed

- **The app now opens on computers where Java Access Bridge is on** (used by screen readers and other assistive technology on Windows). Version 1.0.0 stopped with "Failed to launch JVM" before its window opened on those computers.
- If the app ever fails to start, it now says why, in English or French, and saves the details in a file you can send with your report, instead of the bare "Failed to launch JVM".
- The Microsoft Store package now brings the Microsoft Visual C++ runtime that text recognition needs, on computers that do not already have it.
- SaskEnergy, Eastward Energy and Liberty Utilities bills are recognized as natural gas bills.
